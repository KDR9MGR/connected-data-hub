# Swastha — Social media starter pack

Ready-to-post copy for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X. Everything here is written from what the
website already says (modern Ayurveda clinic · prevention, treatment, diet & lifestyle · BAMS-qualified
physicians · English-speaking team · confidential care). Swap in real photos, case details and team
names before posting.

> **Compliance note.** Keep wording to "supports", "traditionally used for", "helps balance".
> Never promise a cure or quote results as guaranteed, and never post a patient's story or photo
> without their written consent. Numbers on the website (years, countries) should be confirmed
> before they appear in any post — none are used below on purpose.

## 1. Brand voice

- **Calm, clear, honest.** "Real care, minus the gimmicks."
- Explain Sanskrit terms the first time (Dosha, Agni, Dinacharya) in one plain line.
- Short sentences. One idea per post. A soft call-to-action, never pressure.
- Palette for graphics: pomegranate red `#c8102e`, leaf green, cream background (same as the site).

## 5. Ready-to-post captions

**Morning routine (Reel #7)**
> Before you reach for your phone:
> 1. Rinse your mouth and drink a glass of warm water
> 2. Two minutes of gentle stretching
> 3. Three slow breaths, in through the nose
> 4. Step outside for daylight
> 5. Eat only when you're actually hungry
> Small habits, steady results. 🌿 #Dinacharya #MorningRoutine #Ayurveda

**Digestion (Carousel #8)**
> In Ayurveda, digestion is called *Agni* — your inner fire.
> Slide 2: Eat at regular times.
> Slide 3: Make lunch your biggest meal.
> Slide 4: Sit down, slow down, chew.
> Slide 5: Warm, freshly cooked food is easier to digest.
> Struggling with acidity or bloating? Tell us about it — info@swastha.org.in
> #Agni #Digestion #AyurvedicNutrition

**Prevention (Carousel #14)**
> The best treatment is the one you never need. 🍎
> Early signs worth noticing: low energy after meals · restless sleep · frequent acidity · stiff
> mornings · constant cravings · irritability · dull skin.
> If three or more sound familiar, a short consultation can help map what's going on.
> #PreventiveCare #Ayurveda #Swastha
